About

An opaque referral network becomes a transparent, forecasted asset.

Kandula is a sophisticated SaaS solution designed for high-volume Personal Injury law firms, turning an opaque referral network into a transparent, forecasted asset. By delivering real-time insights and accountability across a firm's co-counsel partnerships, Kandula optimizes case referrals with smarter matching and seamless integration across diverse Case Management Systems. With deep insights from hundreds of thousands of PI cases, Kandula empowers firms to maximize their referral pipeline's potential and performance.

Every referral, accounted for

Updates without phone calls

Case updates flow to the people who need them, so nobody dials co-counsel to ask where a case stands.

Works with your systems

Kandula is CMS-agnostic, integrating with the case management systems firms already run instead of replacing them.
